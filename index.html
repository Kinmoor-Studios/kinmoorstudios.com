How a project runs

You see a working store before you commit to building one.

  1. Audit

    We test your current store on real phones and computers, then write up what's slow, what's broken, and what to fix first.

  2. Concept

    We build a working concept on a private development store with your real products. You click through it before you sign for the build.

  3. Build

    We build the theme in version control. Every change has to pass the performance budget and an accessibility scan before it goes live.

  4. Launch and handover

    We set up redirects, check every app, and watch the site after launch. You get the code, the documentation, and full admin access.

The budgets we build to

Every design decision has to fit within these limits. The panel at the top of this page measures the same things as it loads.

MeasureWhat a shopper noticesBudget
