What we build

We make online stores, the apps that extend them, and the back-office tools that keep a brand running. Each one is designed, measured, and tested before it ships.

Themes

Shopify storefronts

We design custom themes for your brand on Shopify's Online Store 2.0. Your team can edit every section without calling a developer.

Audits

Store audits

We check speed, accessibility, search, and every installed app, with evidence behind each finding. You get a ranked list of fixes, whether or not you rebuild.

Apps

Shopify apps and integrations

We build checkout extensions, admin tools, and connections to your other systems. They're for when an off-the-shelf app is too slow, costs too much, or doesn't fit how you work.

Software

Back-office software

Our first product, now in private development, brings Shopify payouts into QuickBooks. It prepares each payout automatically, but nothing is posted until a person approves it.

How a project runs

You see a working store before you commit to building one.

  1. Audit

    We test your current store on real phones and computers, then write up what's slow, what's broken, and what to fix first.

  2. Concept

    We build a working concept on a private development store with your real products. You can click through it before you sign off on the build.

  3. Build

    The theme is kept in version control. Every change has to pass the performance budget and an accessibility scan before it goes live.

  4. Launch and handover

    We set up redirects, check every app, and watch the site after launch. You get the code, the documentation, and full admin access.

The budgets we build to

Every design decision has to fit within these limits. The panel at the top of this page measured the same things while it loaded.
