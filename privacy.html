Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This notice covers kinmoorstudios.com, run by Kinmoor Studios, LLC. The short version: the site doesn't collect information about you.

What the site doesn't do

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and loads nothing from other companies.
  • It has no forms that send information to us.

What stays in your browser

If you choose light or dark mode, or pause the animation, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage so the site looks the same next time. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser's settings. The speed figures on the home page are measured in your browser and aren't sent anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web host, they receive technical details of each request, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. They handle that under their own privacy policies. We don't receive or use it.

The store test

If you use “Run the test”, the web address you type is sent directly from your browser to Google PageSpeed Insights, a Google service covered by Google's privacy policy. We don't see it.

Email

If you email hello@kinmoorstudios.com, we use your message to reply to you and keep it no longer than the conversation needs. Ask at the same address and we'll delete it.
